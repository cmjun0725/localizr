What makes it different from Google Translate & GPT

For checking 3 sentences, GPT wins. This tool's lane is the whole app.

🎯

It knows context & register

Is this a button, an error, a checkout step — and is it a consumer app or a dev tool? The same Korean sentence becomes different English depending on where it lives.

📏

It respects character limits

Buttons truncate at ~15 chars, App Store titles at 30. Localizr proposes copy that fits — most translation tools don't even try.

🔁

Terminology stays consistent

If "확인" becomes OK on one screen and Confirm on another, users notice. Localizr keeps a glossary so the same word always maps to the same English.

💡

It tells you why

Every suggestion comes with a short note explaining the change — so you're not copy-pasting blindly, you're building an instinct for it.
